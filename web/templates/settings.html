Paper Trading —
Signal Aggregator

Controls how DualMomentumV2 combines recent LLM headline-sentiment signals into position-size adjustments for each rebalance's already-selected top-N tickers (a ±50% swing around each position's equal weight). This never changes which tickers are selected. Takes effect on the very next rebalance — no restart needed.

Trading Universe

The tickers DualMomentumV2 subscribes to and ranks each month, also used by the headline-news pipeline. Edits are staged here and only take effect when you press Save & apply, which writes the list once and restarts lean-trader. AGG (defensive asset) and SPY (absolute-momentum reference) are required and cannot be removed. A removed ticker that is currently held is sold at the next scheduled rebalance, not immediately.
